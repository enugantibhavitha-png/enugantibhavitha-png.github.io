About Me

I am an AI/ML Engineer with 3+ years of experience building machine learning and Generative AI systems that make it from notebook to production. I hold an M.S. in Information Systems & Technology from the University of North Texas.

I started at Greens Technologies building forecasting and anomaly-detection models, XGBoost, ARIMA, Isolation Forest, on 1–3M banking and retail records. At Fidelity Investments, I brought Generative AI into the Chief Investment Office: LangGraph agents, RAG over financial filings, and QLoRA fine-tuned financial LLMs used by 500+ investment professionals.

Today I'm building Sandera at BalanX Bio, a real-time multimodal AI product that fuses face, voice, and text signals to adapt how an LLM responds, running on a Redis streaming backbone with on-device inference. I work across the full lifecycle in Python, PyTorch, LangGraph, and AWS, from data pipelines to deployed, monitored models.
